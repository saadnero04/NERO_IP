Chapter 07

Pricing, packages and delivery

Sell packages, not hours. Clients understand "12 posts per month" better than "4 hours of work".

Example packages: posts + short videos

These are example starting prices for beginners in many Western markets. Adjust them to your city, your niche and what local competitors charge.

PackageWhat's includedExample starting price / month
Starter8 posts + captions + hashtags150–250
Growth (most popular)12 posts + 4 short videos + content calendar300–500
Pro16 posts + 8 videos + replying to comments + monthly report600–900

Calculate your minimum price

Minimum price = (hours needed × your target hourly rate) + tool costs + 20% buffer Example: Growth package takes 8 hours with AI tools. Target hourly rate = 25. Tools = 20. (8 × 25) + 20 = 220 → + 20% buffer = 264 → round to 300.

Don't stay cheap for too long

Low prices are fine for your first 2–3 clients. After that, raise your price for every new client. Cheap clients often ask for the most changes.

A delivery process that keeps clients happy

  1. Onboarding form (day 1): logo, colours, photos, offers, opening hours, what they never want to see.
  2. Plan (day 2): send a content calendar for approval before you create anything.
  3. Create in batches: make the whole month in 1–2 sessions using your templates and prompts.
  4. One review round: the client approves or asks for changes once. Include this in your terms.
  5. Schedule: use Meta Business Suite or a scheduler so posts go out automatically.
  6. Monthly report: 3 numbers (views, followers, messages/bookings) + what you'll improve next month.

Simple agreement points (put them in writing, even in a WhatsApp message)

  • What's included, how many revisions, delivery dates
  • Payment upfront at the start of each month
  • Either side can stop with 30 days' notice
  • The client confirms they own the rights to the photos and logos they send
Chapter 08

From first client to steady income

Your first client proves it works. The next goal is income that repeats every month without starting from zero.

The four growth levers

1. Monthly retainers

Turn one-off jobs into monthly packages. Three clients on a monthly package already give you a predictable side income.

2. Referrals

Business owners know other business owners. After a good first month, ask: "Do you know one other owner who'd like this? I'll give you [10% off next month] for each one who signs up."

3. Case studies

Turn every real result into a short case: problem → what you did → result (with the client's permission and real numbers only). This is your best sales tool.

4. Price increases

Raise prices for new clients every time you are close to fully booked. Keep early clients on their price for a while as a thank-you.

Systems that save you hours

  • A prompt library per client (brand voice, offers, forbidden words)
  • Canva brand kits and CapCut templates per client
  • A delivery checklist so nothing is forgotten
  • One day a week reserved for content creation, one for outreach

A realistic growth path

5+ clientsHire help or add a second serviceWhen you're full

Chapter 09

How to spot "make money with AI" scams

The AI money space is full of people who make money selling dreams. Protect your time and your wallet.

Red flags

  • Guaranteed income ("$10k/month in 30 days, guaranteed"). Nobody can guarantee that.
  • Income screenshots with no context. Revenue isn't profit, and screenshots are easy to fake.
  • Pressure tactics: countdown timers, "only 3 spots left", "price doubles tonight".
  • Upsell ladders: a cheap course that only exists to sell an expensive "mastermind".
  • "No work needed" / "fully passive". Real income needs work, especially at the start.
  • Recruiting others is the main way to earn. That's an MLM or pyramid structure.
  • Asking for your passwords, crypto wallet or "investment" to unlock earnings.

The simple test

Ask: "Who is the end customer paying, and for what real result?" If the answer is "a business pays for posts, videos or a chatbot", it's real. If the answer is "new members pay to join", walk away.

If you have no client by day 30

That's common and fixable. Check the numbers: under 50 messages sent? Send more. Lots of messages but few replies? Improve your sample. Calls but no yes? Lower the risk with a one-week paid pilot. Keep going for another 30 days.
