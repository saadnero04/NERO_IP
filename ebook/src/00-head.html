Before you start

What this book is: a working manual. Every chapter ends with actions you can do the same day. You will find templates to copy, prices to start from, tools to use, and a 30-day plan.

What this book is not: a "get rich quick" secret. Nobody can honestly promise you a specific income. What you earn depends on the hours you put in, your skills, your market and some luck. Most people who follow a plan like this one take several weeks to land a first client and several months to build a steady side income.
