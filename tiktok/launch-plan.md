Goal: post videos every day that bring people from **video → comment "AI" → ebook sale ($15)**.

## Step 1: Today (about 1 hour)

### Set up the account
1. Create a **new TikTok account** with a new email, used only for this channel.
2. **Username:** short, easy and money-related, **`@aimoneymoves`** (if taken: `@aimoneymoves.co`, `@theaimoneymoves`, `@aimoneymoves_`). Display name: **AI Money Moves**. Grab the same name on Instagram, YouTube and Gumroad on the same day.

4. **Bio** (max 80 characters):
   `AI Money Moves 💸 Real AI side income, no hype | Comment "AI" ⬇️`
5. **Switch to a Business account** (Settings → Account → Switch to Business account). It gives you analytics and, in many countries, a website link in the bio. Personal accounts usually need about 1,000 followers for a bio link, so check what your account shows.
6. **Warm up the account for 1–2 days before posting.** Spend 20–30 minutes a day watching, liking and commenting on videos about money, side hustles and AI. This teaches TikTok which audience you belong to.

### Set up the sales page (30 minutes)
1. Create a free account on **Gumroad**, **Payhip** or **Stan Store** (check each site's current fees).
2. Upload `ebook/AI-Side-Income-Playbook-2026.pdf`.
3. Price: **$15**, with a **launch price of $9 for the first 7 days** to get first buyers and reviews.
4. Product page text: use the ebook's cover, the contents list and the "What this book is / is not" section.
5. Put the product link in your bio (or in a link-in-bio page) as soon as TikTok allows it.

## Step 3: Making each video

Every video follows the 5 beats (see `examples/cashy-salary-gone.md`):

1. **Hook (0–2s):** the money pain. Big text on screen plus Cashy's face.
2. **Problem:** why the usual advice doesn't work.
3. **Domain:** "The best thing you can do in 2026 is… selling AI services to small businesses."
4. **Proof:** real, checkable facts only. No fake screenshots, no invented incomes.
5. **CTA:** "Comment **AI** and I'll send you the details."

**Production, per video:**
- Script: `python -m problem_channel make --niche money --count 5`, or I write them for you here.
- Cashy visuals: an AI video tool (Everygen is connected to this session; it needs a paid plan) or AI images + CapCut animation.
- Voice: AI voiceover (CapCut text-to-speech or ElevenLabs), always the same voice for Cashy.
- Editing in CapCut: big captions, a fast cut every 1–2 seconds, trending sound at low volume under the voice.
- **Format:** vertical 9:16, 20–45 seconds.

**When you post, turn on the "AI-generated content" label** (in the post's More options). TikTok requires a label on realistic AI content, and labelling protects your account.

## Step 6: What to track every week

| Number | Where to find it | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Average watch time / % watched | TikTok Analytics → each video | Under 50%? Make the hook stronger and cut the slow start. |
| Views per video | Analytics | Find your top 3 and make 3 new versions of each (same topic, new hook). |
| "AI" comments | Comments | That's your interest level. |
| Link clicks → sales | Gumroad/Payhip dashboard | Many clicks but few sales? Improve the product page or the price. |

**The most important rule:** when a video does much better than the others, **copy its structure** right away: same topic, new hook, new example.

---

## Rules to keep the account safe
- No fake income screenshots, no invented testimonials, no "guaranteed" income. TikTok removes misleading financial content, and that can get the account banned.
- Always add "Results vary, not financial advice" in the caption or on screen when talking about earnings.
- Label AI-generated content.
- Don't buy followers, likes or views. It kills your reach.
- Don't post the same video on several accounts.

---

## Realistic expectations
Most new accounts get a few hundred views per video at first. Growth usually comes from steady daily posting for 4–8 weeks and repeating what works. One video can go viral at any time, but you can't plan for it, so plan for consistency.
