Chapter 11

Create digital products with AI

A digital product is made once and sold many times. With AI, you can turn what you learned from your clients into products in days, not months.

Why products come after services

Many beginners start with a product and hear nothing but silence, because nobody knows them yet. If you start with services, you learn three things that make products sell: what people struggle with, the words they use and what they will pay for. Your product then answers real questions instead of guesses.

The product ladder

Successful creators don't sell one product. They build a ladder of offers. Each step costs a little more and helps the customer a little more.

1Free lead magnet (checklist, mini-guide, template)FreeBuild trust and collect emails

3Bundle or toolkit (ebook + templates + scripts)29–99Higher value per customer
4Course, workshop or coaching99–499Premium help
5Membership (monthly templates, community)10–50 / monthRecurring income

Price ranges are common market examples, not rules. Start low, then raise prices as reviews come in.

10 product ideas from your service work

  1. Caption templates for one niche ("100 captions for gyms")
  2. Canva post templates (30 designs)
  3. A 30-day content calendar
  4. A prompt pack for one job
  5. Short-video script templates
  1. Chatbot FAQ templates for a niche
  2. Outreach message swipe file
  3. A pricing calculator spreadsheet
  4. A Notion client-management dashboard
  5. A mini-course recorded on your screen

Build a product in 5 steps

  1. Pick one problem and one person. "Gym owners who don't know what to post" beats "people who want content".
  2. Outline with AI, write with your experience. Ask AI for an outline, then fill it with real examples, screenshots and lessons from your own work.
  3. Design it. Use a Canva ebook or template layout. One font pair, two colors, plenty of white space.
  4. Test it. Give it to 3–5 people for free in exchange for honest feedback. Fix what confused them.
  5. Package it. A cover, 3D mockup images (Canva or Placeit) and a short promise: "Never wonder what to post again."

Quality and honesty rules

  • Don't sell raw AI text. Buyers notice, and refunds and bad reviews follow.
  • Don't copy other people's ebooks, templates or designs. Be inspired by the structure, then make your own.
  • Avoid "PLR" packs that thousands of people resell. They rarely stand out.

Key takeaways

  • Start with services, then turn what you learn into products.
  • Build a ladder: free → core → bundle → premium → monthly.
  • Solve one problem for one type of person.
  • Use AI for speed, and your experience for value.

Where to sell: Gumroad, Payhip, Etsy, Shopify and Amazon KDP

Each platform has a different job. Some give you control, some give you buyers. Start with one, then add a second.

Fees and rules change. Always check each platform's current pricing page before you choose.

Simple recommendation

Start: Gumroad, Payhip or Stan Store (link in your TikTok/Instagram bio). Add next: Etsy for templates, or Amazon KDP for the ebook. Later: Shopify when you have 5+ products and want a full brand store.

Set up Gumroad or Payhip in 30 minutes

  1. Create an account and connect your payout method (bank or PayPal).
  2. Click "New product" → digital product. Upload your PDF (or a ZIP with bonuses).
  3. Add your cover, 3–5 mockup images and a 30-second preview video if you can.
  4. Write the description using the 7-part sales page structure in Chapter 14.
  5. Set the price. Optional: a launch discount code with an expiry date.
  6. Turn on email collection and "ask for a review" after purchase.
  7. Buy your own product once with a 100% discount code to test the whole flow.

Selling on Etsy

  1. Open a shop and choose "digital download" when you create a listing.
  2. Use all photo slots: cover, inside pages, "what you get", "how it works".
  3. Title = what people search: "Instagram Caption Templates for Gyms, 100 Captions, Canva Editable".
  4. Use all 13 tags with phrases buyers type, not single words.
  5. Ask every buyer for a review. On Etsy, reviews strongly affect visibility.

Building a Shopify store, step by step

  1. Foundation: start a plan, pick a clean free theme, add your logo and brand colors.
  2. Digital delivery: install a digital download app (Shopify has a free one) so buyers get the file automatically.
  3. Product page: a benefit-driven description, mockups, an FAQ and real reviews.
  4. Trust: clear contact info, refund policy and secure checkout.
  5. Email capture: a pop-up offering your free lead magnet.

Publishing on Amazon KDP

  1. Prepare the manuscript. Clean formatting, a searchable title and subtitle, an "About the author" page and a link to your free lead magnet inside the book.
  2. Create the title at kdp.amazon.com → Kindle eBook (and optionally Paperback).
  3. Metadata: title, subtitle, description, 7 keyword slots and 2–3 categories. Think like a searcher: "AI side hustle for beginners", "freelancing with ChatGPT".
  4. Upload the manuscript and a cover in the required size, then preview it on the online previewer.
  5. AI disclosure: KDP asks whether AI generated your content. Answer honestly.
  6. Pricing: ebooks priced in KDP's 70% royalty range (check the current range for your market) usually earn more per sale.
  7. KDP Select (optional): 90 days of digital exclusivity in exchange for Kindle Unlimited reads and promotion tools such as free days. You can't sell the same ebook on Gumroad during that time.

Launch blueprint

  1. Build interest for 7 days before launch with teaser videos ("I'm writing the guide I wish I had…").
  2. Launch on one main platform with a time-limited launch price.
  3. Get your first 10 buyers and ask each one for feedback and a review.
  4. Turn the best feedback (with permission) into posts and carousels.
  5. Reinvest a small part of the first earnings in a second platform or small creator shoutouts.

Key takeaways

  • Start where your audience already is: a link-in-bio store.
  • Add a marketplace (Etsy or Amazon) to reach buyers who search.
  • Test your own checkout before you promote anything.
  • Reviews are your most valuable asset. Ask every buyer.

The 5-beat video formula

Chapter 14

Your sales page, emails and automation

Views bring visitors. The sales page turns visitors into buyers. Emails bring back the people who didn't buy the first time.

The 7-part sales page

  1. Headline with the result: "Your First AI Client in 30 Days, Even With Zero Experience."
  2. The pain: 2–3 lines the reader recognizes.
  3. The solution: what the product is and who it's for (and not for).
  4. What's inside: chapters and bonuses written as benefits, with mockup images.
  5. Proof: real reviews, sample pages, your own story.
  6. Offer: price, bonuses, launch deadline (real ones only).
  7. Guarantee and FAQ: refund policy, "Is this for beginners?", "Do I need to pay for tools?"

Chapter 15

From side hustle to brand

A side hustle earns you income. A brand earns you trust, repeat customers and income that doesn't depend on every hour you work.

Step 1: Shift from doer to designer

At the start you do everything yourself, and that's normal. As you grow, ask three questions about every task:

  • Can AI or a tool do this automatically?
  • Can someone else do this better or cheaper?
  • Can I turn this into a template or checklist?
Instead of…Do this
Writing one script a dayBatch 30 scripts a month with AI, then edit them
Answering the same questions by handA FAQ chatbot or saved replies
Selling one ebookTurn it into templates, videos and a course
Doing every client job aloneHire a freelancer for editing, keep the client relationship

Step 2: Build a brand people remember

  • One name, one look: same character or logo, colors and voice everywhere.
  • One promise: "Practical money moves with AI, no hype."
  • Show the journey: behind the scenes, lessons, honest numbers when you have them.
  • Consistency: familiarity builds trust, and trust builds sales.

Step 3: Multiply your income streams

  1. Services: your first and fastest income (Part 1).
  2. Digital products: ebooks, templates, toolkits (Chapter 11).
  3. Affiliate links: recommend tools you really use. Always disclose affiliate links.
  4. Courses and workshops: teach what you've done, with real results.
  5. Memberships: new templates and support every month.
  6. Your own affiliates: let other creators sell your products for a commission.

Step 4: Measure and double down

MetricWhat it tells you
Views and watch timeWhich topics and hooks attract attention
Link clicksWhether your call to action works
Conversion rate (buyers ÷ visitors)Whether your sales page and price work
Refunds and reviewsWhether the product delivers on its promise
ProfitWhat's left after platform fees, tools and ads

Step 5: Go global

Translate your best product and videos into other languages, such as French, Spanish, Arabic or German. AI translation gets you most of the way. Then have a native speaker review the result, because small errors kill trust. Publish each language as its own product page or channel.

EXAMPLE SCENARIO (FOR ILLUSTRATION, NOT A REAL PERSON)

From services to brand in 12 months. Sara starts by making short videos for 3 local gyms. After 4 months she turns her best scripts into a "50 Gym Video Scripts" template pack and sells it on Etsy and from her TikTok bio. After 8 months she bundles templates, a mini-course and monthly new scripts into a membership. Each step reuses what she already built. Real results vary: some people move faster, many slower.

Key takeaways

  • Scaling means building systems, not working more hours.
  • Climb the ladder: services → products → bundles → courses → membership.
  • Track a few numbers weekly and do more of what works.
  • A trusted brand is worth more than any single product.
