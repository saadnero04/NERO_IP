# Ebook draft outline (to build together)

**Working title:** *The AI Side Income Playbook 2026: Turn AI Skills Into Your First Online Income*
**Promise (honest):** a step-by-step plan to land your first paying clients by selling AI-powered services, without quitting your job.
**Length:** about 40–60 pages · **Format:** PDF · **Suggested price:** $9–$19 (low price, impulse buy from a Short)

## Chapters
1. **Why your salary isn't enough (and why that's not your fault).** Inflation, stagnant pay, and why a second income stream matters.
2. **The 2026 opportunity: businesses need AI but don't have time to learn it.** What "selling AI services" means in plain words.
3. **Pick your service (5 options).** AI content writing and social posts · AI short-video editing · AI customer-service chatbots · AI product photos and ads · AI automation (emails, spreadsheets). Each has what it is, the tools, starter price and difficulty.
4. **Learn it in 7 days.** A day-by-day practice plan with free or cheap tools.
5. **Build a portfolio with zero clients.** 3 sample projects you can make this week.
6. **Find your first client.** Fiverr/Upwork setup, local-business outreach, DM and email templates you can copy.
7. **Pricing and delivery.** Packages, how to avoid underpricing, and delivering fast with AI.
8. **From $0 to a steady income.** Retainers, referrals and raising prices. Realistic timelines.
9. **Avoid the scams.** Red flags in "get rich with AI" offers.
10. **Your 30-day action plan.** A printable checklist.

**Bonuses:** prompt pack (50 prompts) · outreach templates · pricing calculator.

**Rule for the book:** every income example must be real and sourced, or clearly labelled as an example. No guaranteed results. Add a "results vary, not financial advice" disclaimer on page 2.
